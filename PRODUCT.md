# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Express.js + EJS (existing codebase with server-side rendering, bilingual ID/EN via i18n)

## Users

Primary: procurement officers and purchasing managers at modern retail chains, industrial manufacturers, and traditional market distributors in Indonesia, evaluating plastic bag suppliers for volume orders. Secondary: sustainability officers reviewing eco-certification compliance for corporate supply chain policies.

## Product Purpose

PT. EUODOO manufactures and trades HDPE and LLDPE plastic bags from its production plant in Cigondewah, Bandung, West Java. The website serves as a corporate profile and product catalog, enabling prospective B2B buyers to evaluate capabilities, certifications, and product range before requesting a quotation via WhatsApp or the contact form. Success means a qualified inquiry from a buyer who understands what Euodoo makes and why it is credible.

## Positioning

One of the few Indonesian plastic bag manufacturers that holds four eco-certifications simultaneously (SNI, OXIUM, InSWA Green Label, Ekolabel) and supplies 100% degradable products. Founded in 1990 (formerly PT. Sahabat Baru and PT. Pelita Baru), the company spans three distinct market segments (retail, industrial, traditional market) with in-house production capacity.

## Operating Context

Buyers typically compare manufacturers via website visits, WhatsApp conversations, and factory visits. The website must convey manufacturing credibility, product specifications, eco-compliance, and contact channels. Content is managed through an admin CMS (Express.js backend with SQLite). The site is bilingual (Indonesian primary, English secondary).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product types: shopping bags (soft loop, patch handle), garbage bags, recycled t-shirt bags, anti-static electronic packaging, auto seat covers, food-grade solutions
- Materials: HDPE, LLDPE, degradable compounds
- Brands: PB and Berkah (traditional market segment)
- Custom printing and sizing available
- CMS-managed content: banners, products, news, capabilities, contact submissions
- No e-commerce or online ordering; all transactions happen offline after inquiry

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PT. EUODOO
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (all roles)
- Color identity: Deep navy (#0A192F, #0D2040) for authority, signature blue (#0055B8) for brand, sky tint (#38BDF8) for interactive accents
- Personality: authoritative, resilient, progressive, industrially precise
- Environmental stewardship is a core brand commitment, not marketing decoration

## Evidence on Hand

- 4 verified eco-certifications: SNI, OXIUM, InSWA Green Label, Ekolabel
- 34+ years of manufacturing history (founded 1990)
- 3 core market segments with real product lines
- Factory location: Cigondewah, Bandung, West Java
- No testimonials or customer logos on hand; future work must not fabricate them

## Product Principles

1. **Credibility through evidence:** every claim on the site is backed by a verifiable certification, a real product, or documented history.
2. **Clarity over persuasion:** the site explains what Euodoo makes and how, rather than selling with hype.
3. **Environmental accountability:** sustainability is operational (certified materials, degradable products), not decorative.
4. **Buyer efficiency:** a procurement officer should find product specs, certifications, and contact channels within seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Touch targets 44px. Keyboard navigable. Bilingual content (Indonesian primary, English secondary).
