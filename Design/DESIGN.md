## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a premier visual benchmark tailored for chemical, infrastructure, and energy solutions. The aesthetic merges enterprise authority with progressive industrial engineering.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Authoritative & Resilient:** Emphasizes stability, governance, environmental stewardship, and manufacturing excellence through deep navy structures and clean precision layouts.
- **Dynamic & Progressive:** High-impact royal and vibrant blues punctuate operational throughput, digital transformation, and sustainable innovation.
- **Clarity & Institutional Trust:** Highly organized tabular data, executive overviews, and ESG disclosures rendered with pristine contrast and minimal visual clutter.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Precision
The interface adopts a high-fidelity **Modern Corporate** approach utilizing structured content containers, subtle architectural outlines, subtle glass overlays for analytical dashboards, and balanced geometric typography.

## Colors
The color hierarchy communicates industrial capability and operational transparency through rigorous chromatic weight.

### Strategic Color Roles
- **Primary (`#0055B8`):** The signature brand identity color. Applied to critical conversion flows, active interactive controls, navigational anchors, and brand marks.
- **Secondary (`#0D2040`):** The grounding deep corporate tone. Applied to major administrative headers, footer blocks, data-dense card heads, and corporate governance modules.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`):** Expressive interactive blue, deployed for active toggle states, secondary focus rings, and real-time metric indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Pure slate-charcoal ensuring WCAG AAA legibility across body text, tabular figures, and interface labels.

### Functional & Semantic Application
- **Surface Foundations:** Background layers prioritize clean contrast with `#F8FAFC` as the workspace background and `#FFFFFF` for primary elevations.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle perimeter containment via `#E2E8F0`, creating distinct boundaries without visual noise.
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - `success` (`#16A34A`): Plant uptime, sustainability goals reached, compliant audits.
  - `warning` (`#F59E0B`): Preventive maintenance reminders, inventory thresholds.
  - `info` (`#0284C7`): Regulatory notes, technical petrochemical disclosures.

## Typography
The system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to project an assertive, modern, and readable presence across high-volume industrial reports and responsive digital applications.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Headlines:** Use weights `600` and `700` with optical kerning set tight (`-0.02em` on sizes above 24px) for authoritative headlines and annual disclosure callouts.
- **Body:** Kept at weight `400` with generous line-height ratios (1.5x) to maintain scan-ability across chemical technical data sheets and press releases.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with weights `500` or `600`, utilizing uppercase tracking (`+0.05em`) for category tags, operational status badges, and metadata columns.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system enforces a structured, architectural cadence rooted in an 8px atomic grid.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop (max container `1320px`), reflowing to an 8-column system on tablets and a 4-column system on mobile devices.
- **Gutter & Outer Canvas Margins:**
  - Desktop: `2rem` (32px) margins with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - Mobile: `1rem` (16px) margins with `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Vertical Spacing Scale:** Component internal padding adheres to compact increments (`space-sm` for chips/inputs, `space-md` for standard cards, `space-lg` to `space-xl` for modular layout sections).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics precision-machined corporate architecture: planar, crisp, and tactile without excessive theatrical drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` base layers framed with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Standard state for static dashboards and data grids.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift):** Used for interactive cards and product segment containers.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Menus):** Applied to dropdowns, action sheets, and modal dialogues.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (High Overlay):** Applied to full-screen modals, critical hazard toasts, and system drawers.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The system implements a **Soft (`1`)** shape language that reinforces engineering discipline and executive reliability.

### Radius Constraints
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) roundedness, preserving crisp rectilinear outlines.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Data Panels, Modals):** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Hero Banners & Feature Highlights:** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) for structured framing.
- **Status Pills & Micro-tags:** Full pill geometry (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for numeric badges and live status alerts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background `#0055B8`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.375rem`, font-weight `600`. Hover state transitions to `#1D4ED8` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus ring renders in `#38BDF8` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined with a 1.5px border of `#0D2040`, text `#0D2040`, transparent background. On hover, background renders as `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, text `#0055B8`, padding adjusted for inline layout alignment.

### Cards & Industrial Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border.
- Header bars for metric cards can adopt `#0D2040` as an authoritative backdrop with `#FFFFFF` text to clearly segment operational parameters.
- Hover states on interactive cards shift the border to `#0055B8` with an elevation increase from Level 0 to Level 1.

### Form Inputs & Select Controls
- Height: `44px` for touch accessibility and industrial compliance.
- Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, interior fill `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focused state: Border changes to `#0055B8` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 85, 184, 0.15)` halo.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Operational Badge:** Compact padding (`2px 8px`), border-radius `9999px`, font-size `12px`, bold tracking.
- Color matrix:
  - *Active / Running:* Tinted green fill (`#DCFCE7`), text `#16A34A`.
  - *Scheduled Maintenance:* Tinted amber fill (`#FEF3C7`), text `#D97706`.
  - *Corporate / Investor:* Tinted blue fill (`#E0F2FE`), text `#0284C7`.

### Tables & Data Grids
- Essential for supply chain, ESG, and financial reporting.
- Alternating rows utilize a soft `#F8FAFC` stripe. Header rows utilize `#0D2040` with `#FFFFFF` text or neutral `#F1F5F9` with `#0F172A` bold text for secondary tables.
- Compact cell paddings of `12px 16px` ensure maximum data density without sacrificing legibility.
